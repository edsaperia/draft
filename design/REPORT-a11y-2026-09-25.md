## A correction to finding 8

The first report's finding 8 said **no `main`, `nav`, `header`, `footer` or
`aside` reaches the audit on any epoch**. That was wrong. `header.navbar`,
`nav.toc`, `main` and `aside.queue` have been in the page since stage 8, and
the audit's own payload listed all four. axe's `region` rule was reporting
the few nodes that sit **outside** them, not that there were none. What was
actually missing was the rails' **names**: two landmarks with different jobs
both announced as bare *navigation* and *complementary*, with nothing to tell
them apart.

The twelve `region` nodes that remain are the same three on each of the four
epochs: the dev stagehand's `label` and `#devwho`, and the `alpha-flag`
(`.alphaflag`). None of them is inside the three columns.

## The three loose ends, resolved

Q1394's ruling was built on 2026-09-18 (`<html lang>`, the topbar title as
the level-one heading, the rails named). It left three things undone.

1. **Two level-one headings on birth and band.** Once the topbar's
   `#titletext` took `role="heading" aria-level="1"`, `h1#doctitle` was a
   second level-one heading wherever it showed. It now has `aria-hidden`
   (`design/session-view.html:655`). It keeps its tag, class and box, so no
   selector and no pixel moves. It is never focusable and says only the name
   the topbar's heading already says. **Nothing moved:** against `main`'s
   frozen reference the setup-probe found 172 differences, every one an
   `html hash` on `topbar` or `titlepara`, and **0 `geometry`**. The
   session-probe was identical. `card-audit`'s founding and settled walks
   gave the same rows before and after, with no P2 or P7.
2. **The rails' names outside copy.js.** *Contents* and *What needs you* are
   now `COPY.page.rails.contents` and `.tasks` (`design/copy.js`). They are
   set at boot beside `PAGE_COPY` (`design/session-view.html:779`), and the
   narrow drawer doors' tooltips read the same two strings. The copy-source
   golden gained exactly those two entries. The walk golden did not change.
3. **Finding 8's correction** is this report.

**The instrument, corrected alongside.** A10 and A15 counted `h1`–`h6`
*tags*, so they could not see the topbar's `span` heading at all. At 1600
this showed as *the first heading is h2* on session and closed. It also
meant the double heading was invisible to the audit. Both now read
`headingsInTree` (`design/tools/a11y-audit.mjs`): tags and
`[role="heading"]`, minus `aria-hidden`, a removed role and anything not
rendered, with the level taken from `aria-level` and otherwise from the tag.
A15 now also reports more than one level-one heading. Run against `main`'s
page, it reported **`A15 h1 · 2 · birth, band`** at both widths. After the
fix, that row is gone.
