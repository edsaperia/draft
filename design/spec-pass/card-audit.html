How it measures — and what it refuses to measure

It borrows rather than invents: the founding drive and the snap() shape from scripts/founding-walk.mjs, whose own header already calls what it captures the material a STYLE.md audit reads; the motion stubs, the SESSION.toggle walk and rect() from design/tools/session-probe.js. One window size and scroll 0 on every run.

Three measurements were re-taken by hand in the browser before any of this was written up, because an instrument that measures the wrong box is worse than none — and three of the first draft's rules were measuring the wrong box, which is worth saying plainly:

  • The tab is supposed to move. The active tab grows 8px out to the left, so reading its rect reported the design as a defect on every card. What is promised is that the glyph does not move — the 8px goes on padding-left as well as width, so the content box stays 34px — so the glyph's content box is what the instrument now derives and compares.
  • A tab in a pile is not where its card is. The tabs behind the front one slide up under it leaving a sliver each, so a stacked tab's rect is a position in the pile. Only the front of a stack is measurable; the rest are recorded as stacked and skipped.
  • A charter clause is not surface copy. Scanning card text for engine jargon reported the Hollow Oak charter's own “Ordinary spending on the running of the house” as a T15 breach. The scan reads what the surface says, never what the members wrote.

And every geometry finding was re-run at 1280×900. Two moved with the window and are excluded: a charter card's automatic side margins, and the tab travel derived from them. That check is now the instrument's own --baseline flag rather than something somebody remembers to do.
