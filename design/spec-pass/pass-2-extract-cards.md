## 1. Card kinds

Shared shell, two implementations:

- **Charter cards** (`session.js`): `'<div class="sugg <kind>-open" data-card=…>' + clauseHeadHtml + field + reviseNote + foot + commitRowHtml` — `suggCardHtml` session.js:2493–2628, dispatching on `stateOf(s)==='sealed'` → `sealedCardHtml` (1540), `stuck(s)` → `deadlockCardHtml` (2395), `kind==='draft'` → `editCardHtml` (2107) / `mineCardHtml` (2181), `diagonal`, `race`, `patch`, else quick/insert.

## 3. Composer rules (K1…)

- **K1 The settled card IS the composer.** For any member on any settled setting once motions are live (`composerOn`: `motionsLive() && viewerIsMember() && !isGate && !ansFor && kind!=='personal' && k!=='text' && k!=='roster' && settled && !motionOn && !(amFounder() && !membersHold && founderDirect)`), the card is: what stands at the head, the alternatives as the setting's own controls, the rationale lane, 🗑️ and the route's commit. Picking an option starts the motion — there is no *propose a change* step. (session-view.html:2506–2512, 3955–3981; CLAUDE `motion-controls`.)
- **K2 The founder's direct hand is the pen.** Where the founder holds ✒️ on a setting (`founderDirect`), they keep the setting body and commit with ✒️; where the pen is given up they compose like a member. `remove` never composes founder-direct (2497–2503).
- **K3 A motion composes with the setting's own control, never a free-text lane.** `PROPOSE` (2617–2700) covers every composable key — title, slug, pace, rate, ending, bar, quorum, removal, lapse, authorship, signing, judgments, chamber, machines, policy, invite, remove — as `laneBlock` radios (`PC.laneBarHtml`, `data-mval`, `edit:false`) or `inputBlock` sentences with the value inside them; the bare fallback lane (*The value you are proposing*, 3964–3966) is reachable by no card today. The rationale is always a lane (`whyLane`, 2603–2608).
- **K4 What stands is never offered back.** `lanesFor` / `PROPOSE.policy` filter out the lane whose label `currentOf(k)` starts with (2601–2602, 2677) — picking the current rule is not a change. (Contrast K-PW, where what stands stays as *Chosen*: a two-state toggle needs its other half.)
- **K5 Text composes nowhere.** 📄 is excluded from `composerOn`; the text changes by proposing in the document itself (`always-on-typing`). The settled 📄 card shows *The text the document started from* (`BODY.text`, 3500–3509).
- **K6 The route is read off the value at compose time.** `routeFor(c, v) = c.routeOf ? c.routeOf(v) : (c.motionKind || c.kind || 'ordinary')` (setup.js:889); ⏰'s `routeOf` makes *never* constitutional and any date ordinary (session-view.html:1290); ❌'s follows the 🚪 rung (1256). `motionCommitHtml` swaps the commit in place as typing flips the route (setup.js:892–909; the input handler at 4930).
- **K7 One route, one glyph.** ✏️ Propose (click) for ordinary; 🏛️ Hold to ask everyone (10s) for constitutional; never a bare word beside a glyphed hold.
- **K8 The mover stands at accept from the open**, revisable; 🗑️ Withdraw is their way out and returns the 🏛️ / ✏️ whole. One 🏛️ out per member at a time (`myHeldOut`, `heldOut` disables the hold with *One 🏛️ each — withdraw yours first*).
- **K9 A constitutional motion re-opens the founding question**: no new object — `motionReopen` + the consent control the card always had (`consentBody`); accept / keep / abstain; abstention never blocks; blind while it runs (count only).
- **K10 An ordinary motion is the race machinery whole** — live, the view serves it as a race card and `judge-race` commits it; the page's `motionBody` + Indifferent + ✓ is the dev seam (CLAUDE: *still a dev seam*).
- **K11 Reserved is assent, not silence**: `crownWaits(c)` appends *If it passes it goes to the Founder, who may assent or refuse.* on both routes (3968–3973); the carried change becomes K-👑.
- **K12 A change carries a reason and says who made it**: the founder writes it on the card (`founderSpeakerLane`, *I am changing this because…*, only when `isChange(k)` — a first decision shows nothing); everybody else reads `changeHalf` with `founderSpeaker` (avatar + 👑, unsealed by construction) or the sealed disc (Q530; session-view.html:4035–4088).
- **K13 Always-on-typing intercepts every input**: every clause and every heading carries a caret; the keystroke opens the `editing-card` with that character applied; the gutter tab is the only way into a decision card from the document (Y18). (session.js composer block from 1733; CLAUDE `always-on-typing`.)
- **K14 A selection may span blocks**: the run is flattened with newlines, the edit applied to the string; block type travels with each origin (`headFlags`, `laneBlocks` split after the diff — cards.js:259–290).
- **K15 One site is one candidate, a run is one place**; editing elsewhere makes a second site and a patch in the making — one Propose, the entries cabled (`d.sites`, `pnav` *place i of n*).
- **K16 One draft at a time** (one caret; `draftOf()` singular; `DRAFT_ID`).
- **K17 The edit is spent at Propose, nowhere earlier**; the price is said in words exactly once, on the hold's tooltip; withdraw returns it whole and flies the pencil home; discarding an unproposed draft flies nothing (session.js:3376–3415).
- **K18 The proposal's lifecycle is one row that does not move**: 🗑️ very left throughout (cancel → withdraw), commit very right (✏️ hold → ✏️ Submitted).
- **K19 result-only with a marking floor**: a proposal states the text as it would stand, additions `<ins>`, nothing struck; below `MARK_FLOOR` 0.5 of the new text surviving it states itself plainly (`wordingHtml`, cards.js:244–258); the ⚔️ card forces the floor off (`force`).
- **K20 Punctuation is its own token** (`tokens`, cards.js:167); a highlight never opens or closes on a space (`markHtml2`, `resultOnly`).
- **K21 *(all text removed)* is a pseudo-element**: an emptied block keeps `<div class="lp empty"><br></div>` and says what it is through CSS, never as content the caret can land behind or `htmlToMd` serialise (cards.js:50–53, 281–289).
- **K22 A candidate's text is markdown, inline only** (bold, italic, code); rich mode serialises back through `htmlToMd`; switching view converts the caret offset (`richToSource` / `sourceToRich`). The lane controls are **B**, serif *I*, and **one `[]` toggle** (cards.js:709–720).
- **K23 The lane preserves spaces** (`pre-wrap`; `readLane` normalises NBSP and `\r`).
- **K24 The seed of a new draft is the lane you pressed ✏️ on** (`laneSeed`: `keep` → the clause; `slate:i` on ⚔️; `a`/`b` on a race; the site's `resultOnly` on a patch/quick), and the head's label says so (*the proposal you are editing*).
- **K25 A lane may carry what is about that lane and nothing else**: a radio (*Prefer this / Preferred*, the loudest thing in the lane) and ✏️ *propose edit*; the ✏️ is an **offer** and disappears when `mayPropose()` is false (cards.js:536), the desk on ⚔️ with it.
- **K26 A motion's identical value dedups by equality; a standing-value change mid-race is a ground shift** (engine; CLAUDE `motion`).
- **K27 Choosing and committing are two acts** everywhere: a radio writes the provisional value into `S` (wakes the ✓); only the commit reaches the module (`S.myAns` → `answer`; `S.mAns` → `answerMotion`; `S.draft` → the motion).
