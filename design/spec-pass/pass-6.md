## 1. The rule, in one paragraph

A race's field is its live candidates **and the current text**, a candidate authored by nobody and staked with nothing. The ranking model orders the field. **The text is the candidate on top**: when the top is not the current text, and at least F distinct members have judged that candidate (F = max(Q, min(⌈E/3⌉, 12)) as today — a judgment of it against the current text or against a rival alike, an Indifferent answer included, the author's derived preference counted as before), it adopts at the next cooldown batch. A tie leaves the current text standing. There is no bar. With rivals in a cycle the model resolves by weight of evidence, and the candidate it puts on top may be one a direct majority preferred the current text to — **an accepted property, recorded** (ruling (a)). Confidence stays in the engine for routing, deadlock and the record; nothing a member reads names it.

**Implementation of "on top"** (survey risk 1): `leaderP` in `packages/engine-core/src/races.ts:171-179` is P(m beats the incumbent), which is the right statistic for one challenger and the wrong one for a cyclic field. The leader is **argmax of the fitted strength over the field including the incumbent** (`fit.strengths`, `ranking/types.ts:30`); adoption is *leader ≠ incumbent* (strength strictly greater — equal strengths are a tie and the incumbent stands) *and* `leaderJudges ≥ floor` *and* the existing `leaderMeasured > 0 || soleMemberIsLeadersAuthor` clause. `leaderP` survives as P(leader beats incumbent) for the record and the routing weight.
