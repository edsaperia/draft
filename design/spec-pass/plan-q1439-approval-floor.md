## 0. The defect

The floor counts distinct **judges** of the leading candidate whichever way they judged (R-102; `packages/engine-core/src/races.ts`, `leaderJudges`), and silence is never imputed (R-089; SPEC §8.2). So a judgment *against* a proposal helps it reach its floor and an absence does not: wherever a proposal's supporters outnumber its opponents but are fewer than F, its opponents defeat it by staying away and lose by voting. At a quorum of 100% one member does it alone, for ever — a race short of the floor simply waits (§8.2). Not voting beats voting no.

## 2. The rule, exactly

For a race whose leader (top live challenger by fitted strength, as today) is X, at time t:

- **E** — unchanged: arrived, non-removed, non-lapsed (`eCount()`).
- **An approval of X** — a member whose *latest usable* comparison of **X against the incumbent** prefers X. X's author is one by the derived preference (§3.3), on exactly today's terms (not while suspended; an explicit judgment overrides it). A comparison of X against a rival is **no** approval, of either. Judgments already cast keep counting after their author leaves E (§9.5a), as today.
- **An opposer of X** — latest usable X-vs-incumbent comparison prefers the incumbent.
- ***Indifferent*** — latest usable X-vs-incumbent comparison is a tie: a judgment (the meter counts it, the member is no longer awaited) and **out of the group**.
- **Awaited** — a member of E with no usable X-vs-incumbent comparison, whose period on X has not run out.
- **Abstained** — the same member once the period has run out. **The period is 💤's** (`afterMs`); where 💤 is *never* (or unset), nobody ever abstains. **A member's period on X starts at the latest of**: the moment the X-vs-incumbent pair *as it now stands* became answerable — X's submission, or whatever last voided earlier answers to it (an evidence reset, `evidenceSince`; the text under X's own lines changing, which since Q1441 (stage 1b) is the only thing that voids the pair — a rival joining or leaving no longer does) — and the member's own arrival or return from lapse (`participant-added`, `participant-resumed`). An abstention is undone by answering.
- **The group** `G(X, t)` = approvers ∪ opposers ∪ awaited, restricted to members of E, **plus** approvers and opposers who have since left E (their cast judgments keep counting; say so in a comment and a test).
- **Q′** — the settled quorum read against the group: share form `⌈n·|G|/100⌉` (product before quotient — issue #24's rule, keep it), count form `n`; then **capped: `min(·, ⌈|G|/2⌉)`**, both forms.
- **M** — `min(⌈E/3⌉, adoptionFloorMax)`, on the whole of E, unchanged.
- **F(X, t) = max(Q′, M)**, and X adopts when: leader on top (unchanged) **and approvals(X) ≥ F(X, t)** **and** the measured clause, unchanged (`leaderMeasured > 0`, or the sole-member exception). `leaderMeasured` keeps its present meaning (measured comparisons touching the leader).
- **The meter** — unchanged in form: `closeness = min(1, leaderJudges / max(1, F))`, `leaderJudges` keeping today's definition (any usable comparison touching the leader). It may read full on a race that does not carry; that is already true today and is direction-free.
- **The router's unheard boost** (`routing.ts` ~:343, `r.leaderJudges < floor && !judgedRaces.has(r.id)`) becomes *short of the floor as the batch reads it* — `approvals < F` — **and**, for a member who has no usable X-vs-incumbent comparison on such a race, **the pair served first is X against the incumbent**. Find where the pair within a race is chosen (`bestPairFor`; `spec-check` reads that symbol out of `routing.ts`, so keep its name and shape recognisable) and add the preference there. R-062 stands: an author is never served their own candidate against the incumbent.
- **Setting motions and membership races** (`admit:`, `remove:`, `invite:`, an ordinary `set` motion) ride the same floor and take the same rule — they are races. X11 stands: an applicant's derived preference is not an approval of their own admission (the existing `suspended` rule gives this; pin it with a test).
- **Constitutional motions and blind questions are not touched.** Nothing in `packages/constitution/src/motions.ts`'s settle path changes.

**Properties the tests must pin** (they are why this shape was chosen): (1) a judgment *against* X never lowers F and never raises approvals — it can never help X; (2) abstaining never raises F — silence can never block, only delay by one period; (3) an approval raises approvals by 1 and F by at most ½ — it always helps; (4) with 💤 *never* the rule is a plain approval quorum capped at half of E.
