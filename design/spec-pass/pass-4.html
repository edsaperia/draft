F-E Commit labels are two sizes, split by surface.

Nothing has been changed — this is a proposed fix. The fact: every word-carrying commit on a band card renders at 11.52px while the charter's render at 14px, the type scale's --t-ui. The audit's B6 rule (a button label is --t-ui) flagged only the one label that exists on both surfaces — OK — which is why the first wording read ambiguously; the census then measured the whole split:

11.52px (band): OK · ✏️ Propose · 🏛️ Ask all members · ✒️ Take the pen 🛡️ Take the shield · 🏛️ Take your voice · ✏️ Take them · 🍾 Begin 14px (charter): OK · ✏️ Submitted · Indifferent

Cause, one rule: session-view.html:138 — .setupcard .btn:not(.glyphbtn) { font-size: var(--t-cap) } — shrinks every non-glyph button on a band card, overriding .btn's own --t-ui (system.css:1878). The charter's cards never pass through .setupcard, so they keep 14px. Proposed fix: commit-row labels take --t-ui on both surfaces — scope the page rule away from commit-row buttons. Veto if the band's smaller commits are deliberate.

Questions — Q1103 to Q1108

Q1103 Do switches stand outside CP1/CP2?

🍾's power table is drawn as on/off switches (✒️ Kept / Laid down / Mixed) — the one control on the surface that is not a choice among peers, and the backlog already wants it drawn as proper switches. CP1's block grammar and CP2's Prefer this label describe choices among alternatives; a switch has no alternative block to prefer.

Q1104 Do value-in-the-sentence composers stand outside CP1?

The numeric settings (🌡️ bar, 👥 quorum, 💤 lapse, ⏱️ rate, the address, the title) compose a motion by typing the value into the standing sentence — no enumerable alternatives, so no blocks. Measured: bar|settled offers no options, one number field, rationale lane, 🏛️ commit.

Q1105 The direct ✉️ send's row: “Done”, or the drawn closing ✓?

Where the viewer's own word sends, the act is on the field in the body and the row has nothing left to commit. C4 says the row is “simply done here”; §9.1's own table row says the drawn ✓, closing. Measured: a button labelled Done.

Q1106 Locked cards: a permanently dark commit, or none?

🎩 after 🍾 is locked for ever, and shows 🗑️ + a dark ✒️ that can never light; a closed document's cards do the same. A dark commit elsewhere means not yet (Y19 even names where the pen is waiting); here it promises a thaw that never comes.

Q1107 After-state and disabled labels: state the act, or the state?

Two places label the row with the state instead of the act: a judged card's “Recorded — choose again to change it”, and the blind answer's disabled commit “Not answered yet” (§9's row for that card says ✓ Answer). The 202 ruling put it as: the row shows what finishing looks like; the title says why it is dark.

Q1108 Patch site sub-blocks: cards, or parts of one card?

A patch opens a card at every site; the eleven quick-guests-* sub-blocks measured with no commit row and no radios at all — the head (patch-rename) carries the one row, and one judgment commits all sites (§9's patch row). Under CP7 (“🗑️ on every card”), either they are not cards, or they are missing their rows.
