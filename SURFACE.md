# SURFACE — what the surface tells a member, and what a control does

The communication rules of the one page (`design/session-view.html`), as a matrix — **event × audience × channel × ask × close × persistence** — plus the rules that quantify over it, the exceptions, and the map from the page's keys to the spec's settings. SPEC.md says what the mechanism does; this file says what a member is told when it does it, and what pressing a control does. Reasoning is in `design/DECISIONS.md`; the history this file used to carry is there under *SURFACE.md, the history lifted 2026-09-07*.

**The session-view is where the entire lifecycle of a document lives, birth to close** (Ed, 2026-09-19, Q1466). Everything a document asks of anybody, and everything anybody does to it, happens on that page, and every rule below quantifies over it alone. **The spectator feed** (`design/feed.html`, `/d/:slug/feed`) is a second page and no part of that life: it asks nothing, nothing on it can be pressed, and it prints only what the two spectator projections hand it (engine-core's `spectator-api` for the text, `packages/constitution/src/spectator.ts` for the rules) — so no rule here reaches it, and it is free of this page's fonts and design language, being made to be read across a room.

**The ten principles** (answers Part 5, 1541.51: Ed, *take all ten as drafted*; redesign stage 10 for the first nine, stage 9 for the tenth). Every card on the page is built by one shell from one state (`card-shell.js`, `card-state.js`), and each principle names the check that holds it on every kind, at 1600 and at 390 (`card-audit`'s P-numbers, strict on every card since stage 10):

1. **A card opens in place of the line it is about**; that line is its first line, word for word, still where it was — except a record (the wording it recorded), a multi-place proposal (the place it is showing), a gap (*(no text here)*), 🪶 at the birth (the title box) and 🎩 (its own sentence, 1564.1). On a rule card the first line is the standing rule and wears the pill; on a power card it is that power's own clause, naming its subject. Checks: P14, P15, P20, P32.
2. **Opening a card moves nothing you are looking at.** The card makes room for its label by sliding the content above it upward; the first line and the pressed tab stay put on screen at both widths; what lies below is pushed down; only where room runs out does the first line move down, by the shortfall (6.4); closing and switching take the room back the same way (6.5). Checks: P13, P16.
3. **Every fact has one home**: what stands is the first line; who chose it, the pill on it; how a record ended, the label above; who wrote a signed proposal, its label; the price, on the commit. Each home carries its `data-fact` role, at most one per card. Check: P33.
4. **A card offers only what this reader can do now.** Closed: nothing but 🥂, whose signature also answers every OK owed; what the close cut off stays readable (*Ran out of time*); the powers lines and ✒️ 🛡️ tabs go (👑 and 🍾's table stay); ✋ 🖼️ frozen. A commit for a power not yet accepted is not drawn. Checks: P27–P29, P22.
5. **A control that can come alive on a card is there from the start, dark, and lights when it has a job**; one that can never have a job there is not drawn. A dark commit explains itself in words only where the reason is not on the card (the ✏️ countdown, 🏛️ in use, 🍾 waiting); no note for *choose*, *type* or the bin. **Every enabled control does something** — pressed, it sends a command, changes the reader's draft or moves the page. Checks: P22 (static and driven), P23, P24.
6. **No empty frames**: an empty part is not drawn and takes its hairline with it; never two hairlines facing. Stated exceptions: the reason box always shows on a card that can take a change; a card is never shorter than its tab strip. Checks: P17, P18, P19, P12.
7. **Each drawing means one thing**: the pill marks what stands; a radio is a choice you can make now; a button is an act; ✓ lights accent blue when armed; green means *decided* (marks, the *Passed* and *Changed by the Founder* labels, the passed highlight, a recorded ✓). A block nobody may choose has no radio. Check: P26.
8. **One commit row**: 🗑️ at the left, dark until there is something of yours to remove (a withdraw is the bare glyph); a note in the middle when there is one; at most two commits at the right. A card that asks nothing has no row — tab, outside click or Escape closes it; OK only while owed; *Accept* for a power (grants, 💡, ⚖️). Checks: P23, P24, P25.
9. **The same thing is drawn the same way everywhere** — band and charter, live and record, both widths. Every card has one label above its first line, all capitals at `--t-cap`: what the line is, or what the card asks; every block's label is its first line. Stated exception: the active tab grows 8 px at 1600 and highlights in place at 390. Checks: P21, P31.

The floating 📝 door and the proposal and patch rows are not a principle's: they are `zone-overlap`'s (P30) named exceptions (1541.17, 1569.2 (c)).

**Nothing you are in the middle of is taken by the page updating** (principle 10, answers Part 5; grammar U1, U2; redesign stage 9). A caret, a selection, a half-typed value, an open choice, a press, a drag and a scroll position survive every render and every poll. The page renders by patching (`PATCH.set`, `design/patch.js`): every slot and control carries a stable key (U2), a node that is the same node with new data keeps its identity, and **a node holding something of the reader's in flight is never replaced** (U1) — the focused box keeps its value, the block holding the caret keeps its words, a pressed control keeps its press. W9 is U1's first instance. Guard: `render-hold-walk`.

**The reader contract.** `npm run spec-check` parses every table under a `<!-- spec-check: … -->` marker (§2 `events` and `lifecycle`, §4 `keys`, §6 `marks` and `setup-alphabet`, §7 `wallets`, `sockets` and `holds`, §8 `order`, §9 `picture`) by its header cells, the `| Yn |` exception rows, §8.1's `- **Fn ` definitions (exactly F1–F23, in order, defined nowhere else), §7.2's bold gesture word and the §8 band row naming the Proposals preamble's tab run (against `PROPOSAL_CHIPS`); `scripts/seat-matrix.mjs` matches §2's **Audience** cells verbatim; CLAUDE.md's `SURFACE §n` pointers need the `## n.` headings. Those shapes are frozen: a free-text cell may be reworded, a column, a row order or a heading number may not. **Every rule label** — C, E, L, Y, M, W, F, K, CP — is a citation target from the other documents, the code and the walks, and is **never renumbered or reused**; a retired rule stays as a struck line naming its ruling (E23).

Vocabulary: **audience** — nobody / the actor / one member / every member owed / the membership / invitees / strangers. **channel** — rail entry · clause in the band · card · gutter tab · topbar · wallet · mail · record. **ask** — nothing / OK / an answer / a judgment / a draft. **close** — what makes the card go. **persistence** — leaves the rail / stays in the clause / reaches the record / remembered per seat.

## 1. Rules

- **C1 Whatever the document wants from you goes in the rail, and everything that wants something is a card.** A card opens only on the user's click — nothing on the surface opens itself, arrival included; the next owed record opening on an OK is that OK's own press (C18) — and it replaces the clause it is about, taking its tab with it; the tab you click does not move.
- **C2 A card closes when its answer becomes the document's** — set, saved, answered, acknowledged, judged, discarded — **and stays open while it is still the place the thing lives.** Discarding closes it too: the discard is a finished act and the close is its receipt. **And a click on nothing outside it closes it** (Ed, 2026-09-06): the birth's dead-click test — no control under the pointer, down and up both outside the card, nothing selected — with a card open closes that card instead of pointing at a task; a click on any way into or out of a card keeps its own meaning — **except the open card's own rail entry, which travels to it and never closes it** (Ed, 2026-10-02, issue #168: *when I then click on it a second time … I want to … not close it, but instead bring it back into view as with the first click (because, e.g., you may have scrolled away from it). If users want to deactivate it, they can instead click outside of it*), its tab still closing it — and in edit mode a click in the text is a caret and closes nothing (K31, Y18) — while a click on nothing outside the lifted column leaves edit mode as it would close any card, the draft kept (K31, Q1315). **A card that changes while you have it open stays open and becomes what it changed into** (issue #143, Ed 2026-10-01: *the decision card will remain open but change smoothly into the record card, and the queue card should stay in the same place, just change colour and icon. This should be true for any card that changes while you have it open*): the card keeps its frame and the clause and the tab pressed stay still while its height glides over the card-morph's 190 ms and everything below the head crossfades over `--wash-ms` — a race decided becomes the record of what you were looking at (C18), a race carried into the Founder's park its ⏳ card (E36) and the park lifted its record, a pair re-dealt or a race renamed the same race's next card by a wording the two share, a motion settled its news or its record in the rule's pile, a delegated question resolved its rule, an application decided what it left; where nothing follows, §9's `read` card, its head and label kept (the PR #146 calls, 2026-10-01). **What changes it is somebody else**: your own presses close a card as this rule and L1–L9 say; and the close is exempt — one page-wide re-lay that closes every card, 🥂 opening (call H, the coordinator's on Ed's behalf). The card is known across the change by its **lineage** (`lineageOf`, card-state.js), the one it opened with and keeps until it closes: a race's pair, bare race, park and record share `race:<race>`, a clause's fold `clause:<key>`, any other card its own key. **No fold forms around a record you have open**, and an open fold left with one record becomes it (C18). Under reduced motion the content steps and nothing glides. Guard `npm run morph-walk`.
- **C3 Closing is not discarding.** Every provisional value — a radio, a number, a draft, the title lane, the invitation boxes — lives in `S` until 🗑️ takes it; 🗑️ puts back only what the open card can write, and never touches a value already set.
- **C4 One commit row.** 🗑️ at the left, always live, on every card but those Y20 lists **and the judgment cards** — quick · insert · race · patch · diagonal · the ⏳ judged pair (Q1500, Ed 2026-09-22): their one unsent state is a radio you move or leave, so a choice is undone by choosing another or Indifferent and the card closes by a click outside; the ⚔️ deadlock card keeps its bin, its desk holding a draft (Q613), dark until a draft starts (1541.9, stage 6); at the right one commit, chosen by the act (the table is §9.1): ✒️ for a set of your own (the power tabs included), ✓ for an ordinary judgment or anything about yourself, the drawn closing ✓ where the act was performed in the body (the direct ✉️ and the applicant's cards — Q1105), **🏛️ for every answer to a constitutional question, as for the raise** (Q1182, Ed 2026-09-05), ✏️ for an ordinary motion, **OK** where the card asks only to have been seen. Every control on the row shares one height and one disabled look; a commit wears its glyph alone (T47), and the raise's 🏛️ tooltip names the gesture that works it (§7.2). **An answer's 🏛️ is a click and flies nothing** — an answer spends nothing, so nothing is held; its tooltip reads *Answer* (Q1195 (b), Ed 2026-09-09). The hold ladder — one second, 🪶 ✒️ ✏️ 🏛️ alike, and 🏛️'s `assembly-press` — is §7.2.
- **C5 Opening focuses the main decision** — the first interactive control in the card's own order (the chosen option within a group), never the commit row; focus is never a press; `preventScroll`. A card asking for words puts the caret after any text already there.
- **C6 Four kinds of entry pin for what they are** — 🔥, an unacknowledged decision, a proposal of your own, a prioritisation — what is about *you* — **and the open entry pins for being open.** Everything else stands beside its clause and scrolls with it. What does not fit is not shown and not counted; admission is ranked by urgency and never thresholded; 🔥, the open entry and anything of your own are exempt from the cap (§6). **One unacknowledged text record pins at once** — the next ✔ or ✖ owed you on the charter in document order, counted from where the review walk last stood and wrapping to the top: the card OK would open next (C18; Q1536, Ed 2026-09-24) — **and up to three of the Rules' news and grants** — the oldest three owed in the band, a rule changed over your head or a power granted, in arrival order; the two are separate queues. The rest of each stand as flow entries beside their own clauses, opening and taking their OK there exactly as a pinned one does, the next of the same queue pinning as a pinned one is acknowledged. Nothing says how many there are (Q113, Ed 2026-09-14; Q1532, Ed 2026-09-24, amended the same day: *the cap of one applies to text records only*).
- **C7 Hot for actions, cold for information.** Yellow and pink want something from you; blue and grey tell you where things stand; grey means nothing is asked of you. `--ok`'s green never leaves a card interior.
- **C8 A decision you had no say in is owed an OK; reading is not enough.** Nothing is owed before the start; the lapsed are owed it too; **and a gate never withholds from the seat that set it** (Y27).
- **C8a Everything laid down by one act is one news entry and one OK — except at 🍾, which owes none** (SPEC §9.7 rule 3). The boundary of the group is the act, not the power, and an entry states its whole batch (E9); **the start is the exception** (Q1184, Ed 2026-09-05): its own news (💡 ⚖️) and the settled 🍾 card's list say what beginning laid down, so no release entry is owed for it. Every release after the start is unchanged.
- **C9 No power arrives without acknowledgement, and is not held until acknowledged — and no task is served until its main action can be taken** (Q1328, Ed 2026-09-11: *I shouldn't be served a task until I can do its main action, so until I accept ⚖️ I shouldn't be given races*). An offer you cannot take is not shown — except the ✒️ commit, which stays dark and names where the pen is (Y19); a question you may not answer is not shown at all; and **every entry a race made waits behind the ⚖️ OK, whatever its state** — the pair asked of you, a race other people are weighing, a deadlock, a diagonal, a shifted judgment and the sealed record alike, their gutter tabs, marks and wires with them — while a park (E36), your own proposal (E37, ✏️'s to gate) and the Founder's 👑 card stand. **On a closed document nothing waits behind ⚖️** (Q1479 (a), Ed 2026-09-19): the closed page asks nothing but the 🥂 signature and has no ⚖️ to press, so the record and its tabs and marks are drawn for every member; and the Founder's amendment news (E35), a ✒️ act reported and no race, never waits behind it. **And a closed document offers nothing but 🥂** (Q1541 stage 7, 1541.7 (a)): no grant is served, no door is drawn, no powers line and no ✒️ 🛡️ tab stand in the Rules, no card draws a control but its tabs, and no record draws its own OK — **the 🥂 signature acknowledges everything owed**, the host's fold moving every owed set to given at `close-acknowledged` (no new event) and the page filing every record the moment it lands; until the signature the rail keeps what is owed. Filtered at ingest in the charter (`withheld`) and by `visible` in the band, never at each render site; the capability is part of the charter column's key, so the OK hands the full set back at once. Re-asked on every not-held → held transition.
- **C10 The document reads identically to every reader; only their tasks differ.** The constitution's sentences are third person; attributions name the office (*The Founder …*), never the name, except the founder's own rationale on a pen act, which is attributed by construction. One sanctioned exception: your own row in the members list, marked *you* and closing the list (Y8).
- **C11 A first decision is not a change. A change carries a reason and says who made it** — the founder on the card, everybody else told what changed and reading it. A long value is shown on two aligned lines, never narrated.
- **C12 Blind while running.** A founding question or a constitutional motion shows only the count of answers; a race shows no standings and no direction; a cleared race is never shown waiting.
- **C13 A rail entry carries a subtitle only on a motion** — its rationale behind the sealed speaker's disc, and nothing where none was given (Ed, 2026-09-12 — it was the value proposed, which the card says on opening); a proposal's teaser is its rationale behind its speaker too, the face where the name is attached (K30). **A task, a gate, a grant and news alike are the name and the mark**, the card saying the rest (Q1374, Ed 2026-09-15 — the gates' *Open* and a settled rule's value beneath its name went); **where the entry is a change, the name is its own subject** — the words or the value it changes (M22).
- **C14 The founding runs in single file**, one clause per step, in the document's own order (🪶 📍 📧, then the constitution's sections — §8); **a grant or gate holds its place without blocking and stands beside the current question**; ✒️ blocks; each answer lands as its own fading clause. A member's answer tasks ride the same order (Q619). **A member's page is not paced by the founder's** (Q1372, Ed 2026-09-15: *if the founder hasn't decided whether to delegate a setting yet, how could it be blocking decisions for members?*): a delegated question is served to a member the moment it is delegated, whatever the founder has left unanswered above it; what the founder has not reached is absent from their page, an undecided rule not yet being a rule; the member's own questions cascade among themselves in the document's order.
- **C15 Mail rides the fold** for every event a member is owed off-surface — the verification mail, the lapse warning and package, the close, and exile — the one event whose audience is outside the document (E31), so its link is the document's address and not a login — and otherwise the link is the login. Nothing else mails (Q575).
- **C16 The topbar reads the document · the room · you**: 🪶 and the title; the pulse, the session clock, the quorum reading and the faces of everybody else who has arrived; your wallets and `me`. The row is the register's arrived rows minus your own — yours is `me` — capped, the excess counted as `+n`, and the head count it replaced survives as its title. **A face there says only that somebody is present**: never what they are doing, never which way (§3.5), never whose a proposal is (§3.5a). Before the start the row is the whole of what the middle says about the room; from 🍾 the quorum reading stands beside it, and that count is the electorate's where the row is the register's. Every wallet is a socket, shown to everybody from the start, struck when not held, empty when spent. **And the reading margin says where** (Q1570, Ed 2026-09-30; SPEC §3.5a): the topbar row says who is here, the `reading-margin` beside the document says which block each of them is reading — presence, where, and nothing else, M25.
- **C17 The host's signals: warn before anybody acts, never after** (Q1505, Ed 2026-09-23). The page reads three things about the host, each with a treatment of its own. **The line** — the page knows it has lost the host from the browser's `offline` event, at once, or from **two view polls in a row that fail or go unanswered** (a poll that has not answered in 4 s is unanswered, so a hung line shows in about 8 s): it draws **a red bar along the top**, under the topbar, full width — never a modal, because a member may still want to scroll and read proposals while they cannot submit — reading ***Reconnecting…*** and, only where the cause is known, a second clause: *Your device is offline.* (the `offline` event) or *docs.vote is not answering.* (the host answered with a server error, which proves the request reached it); a poll that times out or drops on a device that says it is online is ambiguous and adds nothing, and nothing third-party is ever probed. While the bar stands **every commit is held** — ✓ ✏️ ✒️ 🏛️ 🍾, OK, the grants' *Accept*, the composers' commits and the withdrawals — greyed, its tooltip *Waiting to reconnect — what you have typed is kept*, its press swallowed before it can act; choosing, typing, opening cards and 📝 stay free, so typed work is kept. The first poll the host answers lifts the bar and releases every commit. It is a live region (`role="status"`) and does not move. **The announced pause** is not a disconnection — the host answered — and keeps its maintenance modal, which stands in front of the bar; **the stalled flag** is the store's, not the line's, and stays as it is (`docs/OPERATING.md` §3, §11). A vote that still fails in the instant of the press is taken back (issue #37) and says so under its card (Y25).
- **C18 One OK per clause, and the OK walks on** (Q1536, Ed 2026-09-24: *if the doc is busy, someone can come back to a page with dozens of green ticks to acknowledge, which is a lot of clicks*; options 3 + 4; the builder's calls ruled 2026-09-25). **A clause with two or more records owed you is one rail entry, one card and one OK** — the clause fold (§9), **labelled *Current text*, the clause as it stands drawn once, at the top** (#149, Ed 2026-10-02: *I think the top item should be titled "Current Text"*): where the newest of them is a ✔ the clause still reads as, it is that ✔ — marked against the version below it, its speaker, its participation line and *Passed · ‹when›* — and is not drawn again below; otherwise the clause marked against the text before the first of them, or said to be unchanged where none of them passed; **every record drawn in full inside it, a hairline between each** (Q1561 (m), Ed 2026-09-26), **newest first** (Ed, 2026-10-01: *the top of the card has the current text; below that should be each change getting older as you go down, with the oldest at the bottom*), **each version of the clause once, not each change** (Ed, the same day: *not [(v0->v1), (v1->v2), (v2->v3)] but instead [v0, v1, v2, v3]*): the original last, as *Previous text* — *(no text here)* where the clause began as an insertion (#149) — no record drawing its own, and none saying *since replaced*, every block below the top being an earlier version by its place — no rows opening sub-cards, no *Back*; **one OK acknowledging them all**. **The fold wears its own tab, a stack of three ✔s — always, whatever it holds** (Q1561 (n)) — at the front of the clause's pile, and **every record keeps its own tab** behind it (Ed, 2026-09-25: *tabs are ok, it's stacks of queue cards that we're trying to fix here*); **a record's tab shows just that record, and its OK acknowledges that record only** (Q1561, Ed), the fold going on with the rest, its card and tab updated — gone once fewer than two are owed. **A record of your own proposal is never folded in** — a ✔ where yours passed and a ✖ where yours lost (Y13), an early ✖ included, keep their own entry and OK — while a ✖ owed only because you judged it folds in; nor are the Founder's amendments (E35, one OK each) or a record held on a gap (M1). Once acknowledged each record files as its own. **OK on anything owed opens the next thing owed in document order** — the Rules' owed news first in the band's own order, then the text's records, then back to the top (Ed, 2026-09-25: *it should go downwards in the document, and when it gets to the bottom it should go back to the top, and include rules news*) — travelling to it as a rail click does, each item keeping its own OK; with nothing owed the card closes as before. **A card open when its race is decided travels to its record** (Q1565, Ed 2026-09-28: *when a proposal passes while you're looking at the card, you should be taken to the associated ✔️ card*): the ✔ record where a wording passed, the ✖ where the text held (1565 (a)), and the ✔ or ✖ of your own proposal (1565 (b)) opens in the card's place, the clause and the tab still — **in place, by morph** (C2's last sentence; issue #143, Ed 2026-10-01): the card you had open becomes the record, its rail entry staying where it stood and changing colour and mark (M24) — and **a record the card you were reading became is read at the travel** (1565 (b), Ed 2026-09-29 17:27 UTC, ruling (a)): every travelled-to record files as read the moment the card becomes it, so nothing is owed on it, the walk does not go on from it, and it closes as any read record does (no close-only OK, 1541.8 (a)); a record you did not watch decide is owed as ever (guard `scripts/repro/record-travel.mjs`). **On a closed document no record owes its own OK**: the 🥂 signature answers them all (C9). **Enter presses OK** on an open card whose OK is owed — a text record, a fold, or a Rules card an OK answers, never a grant's *Accept* — unless the keyboard is in a field or on a control other than the card's own tab, which the walk focuses (C5). The one text record pinned (C6) is the one the walk would open next; the Rules' three keep their arrival order.

## 6. The marks alphabet and the rail

One glyph per entry, the same alphabet in all three columns (contents rail · gutter · queue). A mark says what the document wants **from you**, never what state the machine is in. Kind names are the code's (`markKindOf` in session.js, `MARK` in cards.js); the checker asserts the kind set, which are drawn, which pin, which are exempt from the cap, and that every kind has a place in both orders.

**The palette's two rules are *grey means nothing is being asked of you* and *hot for actions, cold for information* — and since Q1484 a third: red means *this needs you because something went wrong*** (Ed, 2026-09-21: *make the card a colour that suggests something needs to be done (red?)*, then *Red entry, words unchanged*). One row wears it, `stranded`, and no other may take it without a ruling: it is the surface's one red, `--slash` in channel form as `--lc-wrong`, beside its two existing uses — the strike on a tool your role does not hold, and the phone drawer's count — and, by ruling since Q1505 (Ed, 2026-09-23), the reconnecting bar (C17), which is outside the alphabet and says its cause in words. The entry's ground is a wash of it and the ↻ is painted it; the words do not change, and **colour is the only signal** by Ed's choice, taken after he was told that it says nothing to a colour-blind member and that the entry still reads only its heading. Measured on the fixture: the entry's label 13.91∶1 on its ground and 11.60∶1 on the fill, the ↻ 7.96∶1 and 6.64∶1; the next accessibility pass records that the signal is unduplicated.

**Every glyph the surface draws is drawn, from one set** (Ed, 2026-09-14, Q288; widened 2026-09-16, Q1401: *we should convert the subject glyphs and also the wallet and commit glyphs to fluent flat*) — so the *drawn?* column reads yes on every row. A lifecycle mark, a **subject** glyph (🪪 🌍 ⏰ …), a wallet's tool and a commit's button are one picture from the same Fluent Flat set, through `cards.js`'s `glyphHtml` and its `GLYPH` table; the sprite is `design/fluent-glyphs.svg`, generated by `npm run fluent-glyphs` and committed, so the build never reaches the network. **The character survives as the glyph's name** — in every card literal, every `ORDER` row, every table here and in SPEC, and every walk's selector — and a picture carries it on `data-char`, so a reader that had the character still has it. What goes is the platform rendering it. Three glyphs stay the reader's own emoji font: a member's **face**, which is their own choice; the stagehand's furniture (⏩ ⏭ 📬), which is off the design system; and the mail modal's, which previews another medium. Since Q1360 (Ed, 2026-09-15) *drawn* means **from Microsoft's Fluent Emoji, Flat variant, in colour** (MIT; inlined in cards.js), which is what the rule was ever for: one picture per kind, the same on every machine and in all three columns. **Ten of the thirteen kinds; ⏸ and ↻ are the exception, Y22** — those are the set's two *buttons*, a plate with a white shape knocked out, and this alphabet has refused that shape of object since 2026-08-17, so ⏸ keeps its own two drawn bars and ↻ keeps its character. ### 6.1 Rail rules

- **M1 Four kinds of entry pin for what they are, and the open entry pins for being open** (C6). **A sealed record stands beside the lines that descend from what it decided** (Q1333): its span is carried through every adoption and decree since, so a clause changed again puts the record on its replacement, and a clause deleted puts it on the gap where it stood — held open while the record is unread, gone from the margin once filed. The card says *This clause has changed again since* under its head where the clause no longer reads as the record left it, and *removed* where it is gone — on a record built on the one shell, *· since replaced* in its label, above the wording it recorded (Q1541 stage 1, answers.md Part 4 .5).
- **M2 Admission is a ranking by leverage, never a threshold.** Nothing is too unimportant to appear; the rail runs out of room; what does not fit is not shown and not counted.
- **M3 Three things are exempt from the fit cap: 🔥, the open entry, anything of your own.**
- **M4 The open entry's claim on its clause's line is absolute**; entries that cannot fit around it are dropped, never displace it.
- **M5 Pinned entries sit at their clause while it is visible and pile against the band edge in document order.** The flow population steps around pinned blocks rather than hiding under them.
- **M6 Ties at one clause break by the tab stack's order, then leverage — one comparator for both columns.** **Around the open entry too** (issue #154, Ed 2026-10-02: *Why do the queue cards and tabs come in a different order?*): M4 holds the open entry on its clause's line, and the clause's other entries stand above it where the strip puts them ahead of it and below it where the strip puts them after, pinned or in the flow alike (guard `scripts/repro/rail-stack-order.mjs`).
- **M7 Leverage is judgment leverage, with three floors for what a judgment cannot measure**: an unread decision 0.5 (the middle), a deadlocked race `0.9 + 0.1·bounty` (the top), a diagonal `max(urgency, 0.75)`.
- **M8 🔥 is the most urgent `needs` entry that is not ⚔️, not 🌶️ and not chilled**; ❄️ toggles chilled.
- **M9 A teaser is always in the markup and hidden in one case — the flame with its clause off screen — tested on the anchor's position, never the entry's.**
- **M10 The contents rail shows at most four marks per heading, chosen by `KEEP_ORDER` (retention: what must not be lost), drawn in document order, `+n` for the rest; filed marks are absent from it, never counted.** A heading holding exactly one question opens it. **Each mark is a control that opens its own entry's card and travels to it, exactly as a click on that entry in the rail does, named by the entry's title and what it wants of you; the `+n` tally goes to the section, as the heading does** (Q1520, Ed 2026-09-23: *clicking on the icons next to the table of contents should open those cards*; their size as targets is Q1521). **The marks queue rightwards from the rail's edge towards the document, taking nothing from the heading's width, and where a run crosses the desk to the sheet's edge it rides on a small tag of paper, `--bg` at `--shadow-sm` (M21; on the page's own colour until Q1516 (6)); the rail is `--rail-left` wide, 210px, and 300px from 1680px, the page's cap growing by the same 90px so the charter keeps its measure** (Q1384, Ed 2026-09-15).
- **M11 The stack's front is what most wants you (`STACK_ORDER`: priority), and that is deliberately not `KEEP_ORDER`** — ✏️ is third for retention and seventh for priority. **The Founder's 👑 on the Text leads its clause's pile ahead of every mark** (1541.38, plain bug 8; `stackKey` in session.js, Q1541 stage 4): it is the one thing at that clause asked of the Founder, and a pile fronted by the race's 💡 opened the race instead.
- **M12 The strip does not reorder; the pile opens only its front tab, index 0 in both.** Slivers carry hue, not a count, and are inert; the pile is fitted by `fitStacks`, never fixed. **A switch inside one strip holds the tab clicked**, whatever the two cards are — a live card and a record included (Q1524 (a), Ed 2026-09-24; card-audit P11), a record's outcome standing in the room made above its first line like every label, no longer a dateline row inside the card (Q1541 stages 1 and 4) — **and the tab wins over the label's room**: a tab low in a long strip on a phone leaves the new card's label above the glass rather than moving (Q1558). **The active tab has two drawings, split at the 900px line** (1541.53): it grows 8px out to the left at 1600, and on a phone, where the tabs stand flush with the glass's left edge (1541.20 (a)), it highlights in place — its deeper ground and its lift, no growth.
- **M13 Filed decisions are their own pile at the bottom of the open strip, newest first, off the closed gutter; decided-but-unread is not filed; a pile never closes over the card you are reading.** **A record decided but still owed its OK stands in the live part of the strip, and of the gutter's live pile, at its first block (Q1418), whichever card at the clause is open, until its OK files it; where a clause's one gutter tab is a record's door, the door shows the unread record before any filed one** (Ed, 2026-09-24 (the green tab); guard: `card-audit` P11). **Closed, the filed pile is its front tab with one card edge beneath it per record behind it, capped at four — the queue card stack's edges (M20), depth only, no number — and the gutter's record door wears the same edges for the other records at its clause; opened, the pile draws no edges and its records stand as tabs** (Ed, 2026-09-24, from nh2026's Expiry clause: two acknowledged records read as one tab; guard: `card-audit` P11).

### 7.2 The hold ladder

**What starts a commit is a switch.** The commit gesture is **click**; hold is the documented alternative. `COMMIT_GESTURE` in `design/session.js` is the one place it is set, `SESSION.gesture` the one place it is read, and `?gesture=hold` shows the other position by eye. Under **click** a single click starts the same flight, the control is inert while it is in the air (`aria-disabled`, never `disabled`), the act lands when the flight lands, and nothing cancels it: letting go does nothing and a second click during the flight does nothing. Under **hold** the press is the gesture and an early release flies the token home, as the ladder's *early release* column describes. The *hold ms* column is the flight's length in both positions, and the whole ladder above is otherwise unchanged by the switch. ### 7.3 Rules

- **W1 Every power is an object you hold, kept where you can see it, spent by flying it.** 🛡️ is in the toolbar but is not a verb: a refusal is not an act that spends, so it has no hold and nothing flies out of it.
- **W2 A member's power is limited in quantity and unlimited in scope; the founder's is unlimited in quantity and limited in scope.** Per-setting permission is a property of the lock (the ✒️ tab), never of the wallet.
- **W3 The quill line falls at the save**: the pen is issued when the URL goes live.
- **W4 No power arrives without acknowledgement, and is not held until acknowledged** (C9). `may* = can* && member && acked(k)`, and `may*` is the only thing a control may ask. Grants stage behind the constitutional OKs; each animation fires from that press, per member. 🪶 takes no task; `canjudge` is acknowledged but nothing flies.
- **W5 An acknowledgement covers this holding**: dropped only on a seen held → not-held transition, keyed by seat; re-asked on every not-held → held.
- **W6 Every acknowledgement that confers a power persists** — `ACK_KEYS` ⊃ `GRANT_KEYS`, one key per document and seat, live only.
- **W6a And so does an acknowledgement that confers none** — the 🪪 `pen` admit news, keyed `adm:<applicant>`. It confers no power, so W6 does not reach it; **C8 does** — a decision you had no say in is owed an OK, and an OK that comes back after every reload asks the same member to acknowledge the same joiner for ever. One key per joiner, so it is a family rather than a literal in `ACK_KEYS`: the page asks `ackPersists`. → why: Q912 (a).
- **W7 The pen blocks the founding order; no other grant or gate does** (Y4). The ✒️/🛡️ power tabs commit without the pen's acknowledgement (Y7).
- **W8 A grant says who gave it, in the office's name, third person, read from the record — never inferred.**
- **W9 Nothing rebuilds under a press** — U1's first instance (the opening's principle 10): a control mid-press or mid-flight is patched, never replaced, and keeps its press through every render and poll; the poll still waits for the assembly, a travel and the task sheet's drag, and a deploy's reload waits for every gesture (`gestureInFlight`). The hold belongs to the open card and re-finds its control. A completed hold clicks for you, and that click must not look like the user's.
- **W10 Nothing may infer a power from the DOM. The ghost must never become a removal.**
- **W11 The toolbar is a toolbar and every wallet is a socket** (C16): all shown from the start; `notheld` ≠ `empty`; only `notheld` is struck; `--slash` is the only red on the surface; 24 px sockets; the navbar's height is load-bearing. The ✏️ socket is hidden outright during the storm; the closed page hides the sockets rather than striking them.
- **W12 A wallet says how many, and the pen's answer is ∞**, in the count slot, as text.
- **W13 A wallet must not depend on its own animation finishing** — every flight has a safety net.
- **W14 🍾 has no wallet; the cork flies** (Y11). Every wallet flies out at the close, from the 🥂 OK.
- **W15 One 🏛️ out per member at a time**, returned whole.
- **W16 A hold is released by letting go, and by nothing else**: `pointerup` and `pointercancel`, bound once on the document, never `pointerleave` and never per control; the commit is resolved by **id** rather than by node, which is what both gesture positions are built on. Under the click gesture there is no release at all, so W16 governs the hold position (§7.2). ## 9. The card kinds, the commit row, the composer

**One shell** (Q1541 stage 1; design/redesign/): `card-shell.js` builds a card from one value, `card-state.js`'s `CardState`, on both sides of the page — the label above the first line, the first line, a fact line, the body, the blocks, the row, each drawn only when it has something in it. **An opened card on the shell makes its label's room by sliding the content above it up**, the scroll moving with it in the same frame, so the first line and the tab pressed stay where they were on the glass; where the page cannot scroll that far — its top, or a line so close under the topbar that the label would land under it — the first line moves down by the shortfall; closing and switching take the room back by the same rule (answers.md 1541.44, Part 6.4, 6.5) — **and where the page cannot give the whole room back on a close, the first line rises by the shortfall and the clause lands at home, never held on the glass by a later scroll** (Ed, Q1560 (3); guard `scripts/repro/close-no-room.mjs`). **Every card on the page is built on it** since stage 10 (the stages, 1–8, are in `design/redesign/BUILD.md`; 📭 and 🥾 were the last), and a card whose subject leaves the page while it stands open is a *read* card — its label, its line, one sentence, no row. **A switch inside one strip holds the tab pressed rather than the new card's label** (Q1558). Guard: `card-audit`'s P13–P34, strict on every card (`--kinds=GRAMMAR_KINDS`), P22 driven by real presses.

**A settings card is titled by its ask while it is outstanding and by its noun once it is settled; the tab and the rail follow the card.** The ask (`t`) is an imperative or a question and says what kind of answer is wanted (STYLE T2); the noun (`n`) is what the rule is called. The noun shows in `done` and in `news` — a decided rule that owes you an OK offers no choice, and an ask would say it did — the ask in `ask`, `wait` and `yours`. `labelOf` in setup.js is the one reader: the tab's name and tooltip, the rail entry and a card head take their label from it, and no title literal stands anywhere but `design/copy.js`. **The rail's one exception is a rule change** (M22, Q1523): its entry reads the rule's glyph and value, old → new, from the surface's `railTitle`, and its tab and tooltip keep `labelOf`'s label. ### 9.1 The commit-row grammar

**Five rules over the whole table** (Ed, 2026-09-02, card review — Q1153, Q1154, Q1155/Q1171 — and the QA of 2026-09-02 pm — Q1173, Q1174; the fifth Ed, 2026-09-16, Q1399):

- **A body holds choices; an action is the row's** (Q1399, Ed: *avoid body buttons that are an action rather than a choice — e.g. radios, toggles*). A radio, a toggle, a picker's own control may stand in the body; a button that *does* something — sends, submits, opens, withdraws — stands in the commit row and nowhere else. The email cards' *Send the link* were the last two.

- **A glyph commit is flat when inert and lifted when armed; every OK is blue.** No fill and no accent border on a commit offering an act — the armed state is said by **elevation**, the design system's own rule (*open is said by depth, not by outline*), and the greyed glyph is what says inert; ✓'s solid green stays, green meaning *decided* and nothing else. **OK is the exception** (Q1174): a word-OK acknowledges or closes rather than acting, and wears the solid accent with white ink, everywhere (`okbtn`).
- **A hairline earns its place** (Q1173). The head↔field rule draws only under a head with content over real controls: a card with no head draws none — **an empty placeholder head is no head, so the birth's 🪶 draws none either** (card review 2026-09-05, A5) — a card that is only a head and some text (the grants) draws none, and a settled rule drawn as the first block carries its own border instead. The record card, having no head, draws none of its own (Q1186). **Two hairlines with nothing between them never appear.**
- **The pair groups at the far right.** 🗑️ stays at the very left; where a card offers the founder's power beside the route's commit (K29), the two stand together at the right, the power immediately left of the route's commit — ✒️ ✏️, and 🛡️ ✒️ on a 👑 question. Indifferent keeps its place with the option blocks (CP4).
- **A button offering an act wears its glyph alone; one reporting what already happened says so in words** (STYLE T47). ✏️ Propose → ✏️, 🍾 Begin → 🍾, 🏛️ Ask all members → 🏛️, ✓ Answer → ✓; **✏️ Submitted** keeps its word, the mover's 🗑️ on both motion cards is the glyph alone (Ed, 2026-09-05), and **OK** keeps its word, having no glyph.
- **And the glyph it wears is drawn** (Q1401), from the same Fluent Flat set as the marks, through `glyphHtml`. `.btn.glyphbtn` goes on stating the one size and `.gl` follows it at 1em, so a commit is the same size in every state on every card (B6); the held state transforms the button and never the picture. **The ✓ is the exception and stays cards.js's stroked `TICK`** — a commit is pressed, greys while nothing is chosen and lights on `--primary` when armed, none of which a colour picture can do (Q1360's own ruling). A control is identified by `data-gl`, never by its text: a picture puts nothing in `textContent`, and `glyphTextOf` is what hands the character back to anything that needs to read it.

### 9.2 Composer rules

- **K1 The settled card is the composer**: what stands at the head, the alternatives as the setting's own controls, a rationale lane, 🗑️ and the route's commit. Picking an option starts the motion.
- **K2 The founder's direct hand is the pen**; where it is given up they compose like a member — and where they compose nothing (a clerk) the card is the settled one, read-only. **And where they hold it they may put the change to the room instead**: the pen and the route's own commit, side by side (K29). A clerk composes nothing and keeps the pen alone, with a sentence saying why. **On a closed document nobody composes and the founder's hand is off every setting**: the tabs open the settled card and nothing on it commits — and by CP9 nothing on it *shows* a commit either, the row being 🗑️ alone — and since stage 7 (Q1541) the closed page draws every card with no row but 🥂's, **no powers line and no ✒️ 🛡️ tab** (`closedBlocks`, `pwGone`). 🥂 is the exception, its OK being the signature and `acknowledgeClose` the one command the close does not refuse.
