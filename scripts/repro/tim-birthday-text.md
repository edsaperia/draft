# Tim's Birthday Plan

## The idea

Tim turns another year older on the last Saturday of the month, and the eight of us are going to make a day of it. This document is the plan, and anybody may propose a change to any part of it.

We keep it a surprise until Tim walks through the door. Nobody tells Tim anything, however much he asks.

## The day

The party starts at 4 pm and runs until whenever the last person leaves. Guests should arrive by 3:30 pm so that everybody is hidden before Tim gets there.

The venue is Sam's flat, because it has the biggest kitchen and a balcony. If it rains, the balcony plan moves indoors.

Tim must be brought to the venue by somebody he trusts, on a pretext he will believe. Zoe has volunteered to be the pretext.

## Food and drink

Dinner is a big shared table rather than a sit-down meal. Everybody brings one dish, and the dishes are agreed here in advance so that we do not end up with six salads.

There must be at least one vegetarian main and one dessert that is not the cake.

The cake is chocolate, because Tim always chooses chocolate. It has to be big enough for 12 people, with candles.

Drinks are a shared pool. Everybody puts in 15 pounds and Leo does one shop for all of it.

## The present

We buy one joint present rather than eight small ones. The budget is 20 pounds each.

Tim has mentioned wanting a decent pair of walking boots, a record player and a weekend away. We should pick one of these and not improvise.

The card is signed by everybody, and anybody who cannot come signs it in advance.

## Music and games

Priya makes the playlist. Requests go to her by the Wednesday before, and she may refuse anything with a saxophone solo.

There is one organised game after the cake, and it should be short. Charades is the default unless somebody proposes something better.

Speeches are optional and no speech may run longer than three minutes.

## Afterwards

Whoever is still standing at the end helps Sam tidy up. The recycling goes out that night.

Photos go into one shared album within a week, and nothing goes online without Tim seeing it first.
