# The Residency Charter
## Purpose
The residency exists to put technologists in the same building as the people politics is done to, for long enough that both are changed by it.
A resident is here to build something in public. Talking about building is not building.
## What we make
Everything made in the residency is released under an open licence, without exception.
Residents keep the copyright in what they make; the residency takes none of it.
Residents may use AI tools to draft, research and code, but every published word is read by a person before it goes out.
## Money
The residency takes no money from political parties, and none from any government.
Every source of funding, and its amount, is published on the wiki within a month of receipt.
## How we live
What is said in the house may be repeated outside it; who said it may not.
Residents eat together on Tuesdays. Attendance is expected, not enforced.
A resident who has shipped nothing in public for three months is asked to leave.
